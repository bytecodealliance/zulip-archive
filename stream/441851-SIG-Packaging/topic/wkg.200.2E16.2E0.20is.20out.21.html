wkg@0.16.0 is out now!
https://github.com/bytecodealliance/wasm-pkg-tools/releases/tag/v0.16.0

This includes:

  • intial wkg workspace support
  • multi package publish (works with workspaces as well: wkg publish --workspace)
  • semver validation on publish
  • wkg publish --dry-run
  • updated docs
  • per registry authentication envvars
  • and more!

Still more to do but huge shoutout to @Victor Adossi and @Ryan P for their contributions/help!
