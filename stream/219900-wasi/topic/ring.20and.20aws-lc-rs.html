bjorn3 (Sep 23 2026 at 11:26):

Both ring and aws-lc-rs need a C compiler (in the case of wasi it needs a C compiler that can compile to wasm, so you must make sure it uses clang (preferably from wasi-sdk), not msvc or gcc). I'm not sure to what extent either of them supports compiling for wasm. They tend to use crypto routines written in assembly by default, which isn't portable across architectures.
