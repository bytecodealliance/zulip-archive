Luke Wagner (Sep 23 2026 at 17:35):

Hello everyone, and excited to start meeting as a SIG! To recap: this is a meeting dedicated to providing feedback and guidance to the Component Model specification based on the Bytecode Alliance's producer and consumer toolchain implementations. So it's a good meeting to raise issues with what's already merged in the CM spec as well as what's being proposed in PRs/Issues from a BA perspective. It's also a good venue to coordinate CM-related BA implementation work between different projects. For pure spec discussions and anything WASI-related, there has traditionally been the WASI SG biweekly hosted inside the WebAssembly CG and there will likely soon be a new Component Model-scoped SG biweekly too with a more browser focus (and of course the CM spec GH repo).

Meeting information with zoom and agenda links: https://github.com/bytecodealliance/meetings/tree/main/SIG-Component-Model

The agenda is currently just a public-edit gdoc so to add an agenda item, just add a bullet to the next date.

See you tomorrow!
