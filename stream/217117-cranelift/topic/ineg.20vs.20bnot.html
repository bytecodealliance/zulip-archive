Topic: ineg vs bnot

Firestar99 (Aug 04 2026 at 15:09):

Am I missing something, or what's the difference between ineg and bnot? With ineg being two's complement aka. a bitwise invert, shouldn't they both be the same operation?

In aarch64 lowering, I see ineg lowered into 0 - x whereas bnot turns into orr_not(...). I'd guessisub affects processor flags for eg. nz, overflow, whereas bnot doesn't. But flags aren't modelled in clif, so why make the difference on that level?

Chris Fallin (Aug 04 2026 at 15:14):

Two's complement negation is ~x + 1 (negation and bitwise invert are equivalent in ones-complement) -- so they're distinct
