cfallin opened PR #15228 from cfallin:gc-maybe-rooted to bytecodealliance:main:

This introduces MaybeRooted<T>, an enum of the ways that a Val or Ref may hold a reference to a GC object, and makes the GC-reference variants of Val (ExternRef, AnyRef, ExnRef) and of Ref (Extern, Any, Exn) hold Option<MaybeRooted<T>> instead of Option<Rooted<T>>.

For now MaybeRooted<T> has only one variant, MaybeRooted::Rooted, which wraps a Rooted<T> as before, so this is a pure refactor with no change in behavior. The point is to give Val room for other kinds of GC references, as we will soon do with a followup that adds unrooted references.
