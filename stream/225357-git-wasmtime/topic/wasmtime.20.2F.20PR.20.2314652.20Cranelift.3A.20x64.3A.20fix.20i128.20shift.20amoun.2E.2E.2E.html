cfallin opened PR #14652 from cfallin:fix-i128-shift-amounts to bytecodealliance:main:

In several lowering rules that match on patterns around shifts, an i128-sized shift amount caused a compiler panic because its representation consists of multiple registers. Shifts in Cranelift always mask the shift amount (to a power-of-2) so taking the low 64-bit register of the shift amount is correct/lossless and avoids the panic.
