When consume_fuel is enabled, FuncEnvironment caches the in-flight fuel counter in a function-local Cranelift SSA Variable (self.fuel_var) and synchronizes it with VMStoreContext::fuel_consumed across calls, returns, and exception handlers.

Previously, stack-switching instructions (resume, resume_throw, resume_throw_ref, suspend, and switch) did not flush/save self.fuel_var before switching stacks or reload self.fuel_var upon returning or resuming, and translate_resume_impl did not reload self.fuel_var at the entry of suspend_block before dispatching to (on $tag $label) handler blocks. As a result, fuel consumed inside a continuation was overwritten whenever control switched back to the parent or target stack.

Save self.fuel_var in fuel_before_op prior to resume*, suspend, and switch, reload self.fuel_var in fuel_after_op on normal return/wake-up, and call env.on_catch_block_entry(builder) at the start of suspend_block.
