This commit fixes an issue cropping up on CI where it appears that the latest version of kernels in use have tweaked the behavior of the PAGEMAP_SCAN ioctl. Specifically WRITTEN may show up for pages that are in fact not actually written. The fix here is to, in addition to other categories already required, to require pages are either present or swapped out. This seems to work across both older and newer kernels.
