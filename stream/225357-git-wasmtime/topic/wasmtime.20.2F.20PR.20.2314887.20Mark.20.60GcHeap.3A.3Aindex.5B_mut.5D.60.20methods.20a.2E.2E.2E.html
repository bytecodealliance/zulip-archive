fitzgen opened PR #14887 from fitzgen:heap-index-inline-always to bytecodealliance:main:

Even though they are generic and marked #[inline] they aren't always getting inlined when you would expect them to be, but it is critical for collector performance that they are inlined. It turns out that enabling LTO isn't even good enough to inline them reliably, we were really just getting lucky with the exact codegen unit slicing we landed on for a while that they were being inlined at all.

So instead, just mark them as inline(always).

Fixes https://github.com/bytecodealliance/are-we-fast-yet/issues/7
