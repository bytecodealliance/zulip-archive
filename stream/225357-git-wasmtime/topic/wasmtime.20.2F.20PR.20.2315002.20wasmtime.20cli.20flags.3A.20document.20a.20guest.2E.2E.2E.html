It turns out that even SIGTERM can be avoided by a looping wasm guest, because in some situations in wasmtime serve it can tokio worker to spin forever when there are no timeouts available to interrupt it.

The options to this are either to make the overhead of epoch mandatory, or to accept that this is a way for a wasm to consume CPU until things escalate to SIGKILL. For wasmtime cli, I think its more valuable to retain the default of running without timeouts, and just document that this is a possible shortcoming.
