This patch adds some defense-in-depth measure to prevent the engine from segfaulting due to stack overflow on the initial entry to a high-arity continuation.

The principal idea is to calculate the required headroom for the continuation's persistent control data. I have deliberately opted for a runtime calculation rather than compile-time calculation for simplicity and to keep the changes local to the stack-switching feature. In principle, the result could be attached as metadata to the compiled artifact.

Resolves #13020.
