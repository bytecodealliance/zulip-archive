Second of a series splitting https://github.com/bytecodealliance/wasmtime/pull/14696
into one PR per instruction class, following https://github.com/bytecodealliance/wasmtime/pull/14930.
Adds the APX NDD (new data destination) forms of the unary ops neg and not, which
use a VM encoding where the written operand takes the vvvv slot and ModRM.reg still
holds the opcode extension, plus the 32-bit RVM forms of add, sub, and, or and xor.
i8 and i16 lower through the 32-bit form, since they are already widened. has_apx is off by default, so nothing changes unless it's
enabled. More NDD patches to follow.
