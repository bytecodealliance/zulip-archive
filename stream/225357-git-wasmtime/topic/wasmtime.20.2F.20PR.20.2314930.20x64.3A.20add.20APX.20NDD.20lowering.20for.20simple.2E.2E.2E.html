jlb6740 opened PR #14930 from jlb6740:apx-patch-lower-simple-binary-ops to bytecodealliance:main:

First of a series splitting #14696 into one PR per instruction class. Adds the
APX NDD (new data destination) forms of sub, and, or and xor for 64-bit
register-register ops, following the pattern #13916 established for add: an
RVM encoding promoted into EVEX map 4 with ND = 1, plus a lowering rule
guarded on use_apx. The separate destination means the register allocator no
longer needs a move to preserve a live input. has_apx is off by default, so
nothing changes unless it's enabled. More NDD patches to follow.
