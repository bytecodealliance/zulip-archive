Add a bunch of mid-end ISLE optimizations whose left-hand sides were harvested from spidermonkey.wasm, right-hand sides synthesized by Souper, and then some of them were additionally generalized by Hydra.

This skips the icmp-related rules from that issue, as they introduced redundant computations since they optimized away values that were reused elsewhere, and so they just became partially computed twice, and our egraph rewrites / extraction doesn't reason about reuse. (This redundancy resulted in some minor code pessimizations and slowdowns on Sightglass.)

No statistically significant changes on Sightglass.

Fixes #5783
