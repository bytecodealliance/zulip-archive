The contract makes this future the place for "trailers received, or an error", but on a body error it reports "no trailers".
This PR sends the error into that future, and adds a test.
Here I choose to leave the real error to the read path; what goes to the future is a synthesized Error::HttpProtocolError, because that keeps the change surface smallest. But the real error could also be sent to the future as a whole, with the read path changed to return the category error.
