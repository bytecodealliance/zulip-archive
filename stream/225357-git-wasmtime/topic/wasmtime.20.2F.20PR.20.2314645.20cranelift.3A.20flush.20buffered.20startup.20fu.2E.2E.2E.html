During FuncTranslator::translate_module_startup, fuel costs from earlier startup instructions (such as global.init constant expressions) are buffered in self.fuel_consumed until flushed into the SSA self.fuel_var.

In FuncEnvironment::translate_static_memory_init, the copy-on-write check branches directly to end_block (brif(is_null, end_block, init_block)), while self.fuel_consumed was only flushed inside init_block via fuel_consume_for_bulk_op. When the CoW fast path was taken (is_null != 0), init_block was skipped and the buffered compile-time startup fuel was lost.

Call self.fuel_increment_var(builder) before brif(is_null, end_block, init_block) when consume_fuel is enabled so both arms of the branch see the prior startup fuel.
