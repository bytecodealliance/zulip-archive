Chris Fallin (Oct 09 2026 at 15:46):

Great question! I structured that particular feature as a generic hook in the engine plus the actual slot allocation logic in NightMonkey (separate repo) -- so it was at least designed to try to be palatable to upstream. The invariant is suprisingly not used in that many places and I was able to generalize sufficiently to make this work.
